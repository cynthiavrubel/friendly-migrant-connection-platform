# User Stories

## Account and privacy

- As a new user, I want to register securely so that I can access Friendly.
- As a registered user, I want to log in and log out securely.
- As a user, I want only necessary personal information to be collected.
- As a user, I want to update or delete my personal information.
- As a user, I do not want my private address or live GPS position exposed.

## Profile and connection intention

- As a user, I want to create and edit a profile so that Friendly can understand the connections relevant to me.
- As a user, I want to select the languages I use and the interests I enjoy.
- As a user, I want to add a short biography.
- As a user, I want to indicate whether I am living locally, recently relocated, visiting, travelling soon, a local resident or open to welcoming newcomers.
- As a user, I want to indicate whether I am currently open to connection.

## Home and discovery location

- As a user, I want to set my home country and city so that my normal location is represented accurately.
- As a user, I want to choose an active discovery country and city so that I control where I search.
- As a traveller, I want to change my discovery location before travelling without changing my home location.
- As a visitor, I want my temporary discovery city to differ from where I normally live.
- As a privacy-conscious user, I want location discovery to work without sharing my exact address or live GPS.

## People discovery and matching

- As a user, I want to find people in my selected European city.
- As a user, I want to see only people who are open to connection in that discovery location.
- As a user, I want to connect through shared languages and interests.
- As a user, I want connection intention and relocation or travel context to improve recommendation relevance.
- As a local resident, I want to find newcomers I may be able to welcome.
- As a user, I want recommendations ordered by relevance within my selected location.
- As a user, I want to understand the reasons behind a recommendation.
- As a future traveller, I may want recommendations to consider overlapping availability dates.

## Community Plans

- As a user, I want to create a Community Plan for a casual local activity.
- As a plan creator, I want to select its country and city.
- As a plan creator, I want to provide a title, category, description, date and time.
- As a plan creator, I want to set an optional capacity.
- As a user, I want to browse Community Plans by location.
- As a user, I want to view a plan's details before joining.
- As a user, I want to join a plan.
- As a participant, I want to leave a plan when my circumstances change.
- As a creator, I want to edit my own plan.
- As a creator, I want to cancel my own plan.
- As a user, I want to view plans I created.
- As a user, I want to view plans I joined.

## Safety and ownership

- As a user, I want only the creator to edit or cancel their Community Plan.
- As a user, I want plan capacity and participation changes to remain consistent.
- As a user, I want clear information about what is public before submitting profile or plan data.
- As a user, I want inappropriate users or plans to be reportable in a later safety milestone.
